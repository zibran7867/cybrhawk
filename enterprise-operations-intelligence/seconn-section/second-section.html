Implementation and operations are separate cost categories

Initial ingestion, connectors and customization are scoped per project. Operations may add infrastructure, AI, communications, support and specialized knowledge. Account ownership, payment responsibility, limits and spending alerts are agreed in advance.

Cost summary: what to budget for

Supabase and Cloudflare carry costs in the planned architecture. Separate the cost of operating HawkOps from what the commercial fee includes: an existing cost is not always a separate additional charge.
